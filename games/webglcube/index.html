Keyboard Controls:

Arrow keys: Rotate cube X/Y axes

J/L: Spin cube counter-clockwise/clockwise (Z axis)

W/S: Move cube up/down

A/D: Move cube left/right

I/K: Move cube forward/backward (Z axis)

Touch Controls (Mobile):

Swipe left/right: Rotate left/right

Swipe up/down: Rotate up/down

Pinch in (expand): Move cube towards viewer

Pinch out (contract): Move cube away from viewer

Face Colors & Letters:

Red (A), Orange (B), Yellow (C), Green (D), Blue (E), Purple (F)
